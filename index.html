NICKEL KNOCK

Set up your table

Pass the device between friends. You control the seats you pick; the rest stay bots. Hands stay hidden between turns.

When a player runs out of lives they get one extra life, shown as a blue chip with a C.

Players pay for their lives from their own money. The last player standing wins money for every life bought in the game. Extra lives can be bought between rounds, and the price climbs fast. Bots get a random spending personality each game: hover over a bot to see theirs.

Nickel Knock rules

Build the best hand of three cards, then lose as few lives as you can. The last player with lives wins.

Card values

Ace 11, J/Q/K 10, number cards their number. Jokers are worth 0. Your score is the total of your best single suit. Three of a kind (like three 9s) is the three values plus 0.5 (27.5), regardless of suit.

Instant wins

  • Triple Aces (best), then 31 (Ace plus two 10-value cards of one suit), then Double Joker.
  • Everyone except the winner loses a life. Two 31s: both are safe. All hands are shown, and players one card away from an instant win of the same level get one more turn.

Instant-win odds

Chance of being dealt each one in your starting three cards: Triple Aces 1 in 6,201, a 31 1 in 1,034, and Double Joker 1 in 477.

Your turn

  • Take the face-up card from the play pile and swap it for one of your cards, or
  • draw from the draw pile and either swap it for one of your cards or drop it on the play pile.
  • The player after the dealer goes first, and the dealer goes last. Play always goes counter-clockwise, and the dealer role passes counter-clockwise each round.
  • Hover the play pile to see every card in it.

Knocking

The Knock button works during your own turn and for a short window after you finish it. After a knock, everyone else gets one more turn, then hands are compared. A knocker who ends with the lowest hand loses 2 lives.

Losing lives

The lowest hand loses a life (ties all lose one). If the draw pile ever runs out, the round ends immediately and the lowest hands lose.
